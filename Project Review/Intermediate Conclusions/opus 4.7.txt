Meta-observations
A few things the exercise surfaced about the models themselves, which might be useful for future rounds:

Opus was the most ambitious and the most wrong on verifiable claims. It proposed the most speculative directions (phone-as-detector, aggressive detector swaps, MQTT now) and also made the one factual error (react-map-gl). More imagination, less grounding.
GPT and Gemini are near-duplicates. Their disagreement matrix against each other is tiny — same bugs found, same priorities, near-identical tables. Running both may not be giving you much independent signal. You'd get more from pairing Opus with one of them plus a third model from a different family (e.g., DeepSeek or Qwen) for actual triangulation.
The remaining real disagreements are mostly about timing (P1 vs P2) and product scope (demo vs product), not about facts. Those are your calls to make — the models can't resolve them because they don't know your intent.
